---
title: "Urza's Oculus"
description: "A native macOS prototype for thoughtful, evidence-informed Magic: The Gathering Arena drafts."
---

Urza's Oculus is an experiment in making draft decisions easier to understand.

The long-term goal is to bring reliable draft history, card and format data, and explainable recommendations into one native macOS companion. The current release is an early capture prototype—not an automatic draft tracker or pick advisor.

**The goal is clarity, not autopilot.** Oculus is being built to help players understand the shape of a draft while keeping every decision theirs.

## An instrument for better-informed picks

<div class="feature-grid">
  <article class="feature">
    <span class="feature-mark">01</span>
    <h3>Observe the draft</h3>
    <p>The current prototype explores window-scoped capture from the Arena experience through iPhone Mirroring. Pack recognition and draft-history tracking are future work.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="feature">
    <span class="feature-mark">02</span>
    <h3>Understand the evidence</h3>
    <p>Foundational card, format, and recommendation data pipelines are being built with provenance and explicit limitations. They are not yet connected to live picks.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="feature">
    <span class="feature-mark">03</span>
    <h3>Keep the player in control</h3>
    <p>The intended companion will offer deterministic, explainable guidance without automating picks or interacting with Arena.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Built with care
